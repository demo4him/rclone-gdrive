Privacy Policy

This application is a personal Google Drive OAuth application used with rclone.

The application is used solely by its owner to access and manage files in the owner's Google Drive.

This application does not collect, sell, or share personal data with third parties.

OAuth credentials and tokens are used for authentication and are stored locally by the rclone installation.

Google Drive data is transferred directly between the user's computer and Google services through rclone.

This application is not intended for use by other users.
